## Tour Overview

More than eleven hundred years ago, a Přemyslid duke built a wooden fort on a ridge above the Vltava. Prague grew up around it. Ever since, this hilltop has been the seat of power over the Czech lands: dukes, kings, Holy Roman Emperors, Nazi occupiers, Communist leaders and democratic presidents have all ruled from here. The President of the Czech Republic still works here today.

This tour reads the Castle the way Czechs do: not as a pretty postcard, but as the place where the nation's story was written. Expect saints and murders, golden ages and defenestrations, and the stubborn sense of who we are that survived all of them.

We start at the top of the hill and walk mostly downhill through the courtyards. The tour doesn't normally go inside the paid interiors, but we can add them. Just let me know when you book.

- Old Royal Palace and St. George's Basilica

### Topics covered

- The Přemyslids and the beginnings of Prague
- Saint Wenceslas and the Czech crown
- Charles IV and Prague's golden age
- The Habsburgs, Rudolf II and the defenestrations
- Masaryk, Plečnik and the First Republic
- The Castle under occupation and Communism
- Václav Havel and the Castle today
